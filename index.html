Connect your own account

TikTok integration is in development and sandbox testing. The workflow requests access to basic profile information and permission to upload video drafts or publish videos to an account that has explicitly authorized the app.

Access is subject to platform approval, account permissions, and API restrictions. Connecting an account does not guarantee public publishing.

A desktop tool, a simple public website

This website provides app information and policies. It does not accept social media passwords, collect videos, or offer account sign-in. Account authorization runs through the platform’s official OAuth pages in the local desktop workflow.
